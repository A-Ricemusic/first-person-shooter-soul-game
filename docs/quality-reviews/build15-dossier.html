ONGOING QUALITY MISSION / BUILD 15

A sheltered triage archive with two ways through.

West medical now occupies a high-canopied evacuation bay. Its broad south intake gives an immediate view of the archive; the east service passage offers an alternate entry and retreat. A tall storage spine conceals the medic side of the room, while the intake desk provides low cover. Empty cot, folded stretchers, evacuation lockers and ferry tags give Mara's last-ferry memory a physical setting. East engineering and the Heart retain open sky for contrast. The finite two-sentinel stage remains independent of recovery order; there is no mandatory kill, phase, jump or parkour gate.

The original Blender shell and major furniture are generated from src/medical-layout.json by retained tools/build_medical.py, with .blend, GLB and provenance. Runtime shell collision, encounter spawns, recovery/story placement and weather shelter share that layout. Original station concrete PBR materials are reused. The 7.3m clear ceiling accommodates the pylon and its 6.8m sign top; neither recovery route requires a jump. Story signage is mounted on the west wall, and the east threshold has an interior exit cue.

A static half-metre walkability cache supports bounded A* (maximum 2,400 expanded nodes/query, at most one query per enemy per .8s), with swept waypoint shortcuts and the existing local steering/separation. Direct combat, Interceptor prediction, bounded Warden steering, health, damage, precision and phase/attack timings are preserved. This is grounded navigation with an approximate axis collision solver, not a general navigation mesh or full visual-body physics.
